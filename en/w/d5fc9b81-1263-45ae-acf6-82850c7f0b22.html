Warm Breath of the Wind

Теплий подих вітру

Oil painting, palette knife · 40 × 40 cm · 2025

Inspired by summer meadow landscapes, this painting was created with professional oil paints on natural cotton canvas using the palette-knife technique. The knife work conveys the atmosphere of a flower field and the richness of its textures, while the abstract style gives the painting a sense of movement — like an open meadow where the flowers sway in a warm summer breeze.
