Privacy

Privacy policy

In short: I don't collect your data without your involvement.

What is stored in your browser

This site keeps only your own choices in your browser's localStorage: the cart, the language, the theme (light/dark) and a cache of the halls' colours. None of it is shared with third parties, and it disappears when you clear the site's data in your browser.

What you send yourself

  • The “Commission a painting” form: your name, contact and a few words about your idea.
  • Checking out the cart: the titles of the works and the total.

These messages reach me in Telegram or by email — only so that I can reply to you. I don't use them for mailings and don't pass them on to anyone.

Services involved

  • Supabase — the database and the storage for photos of the works.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting of the site.
  • Telegram and email — receiving your orders and questions.
  • Etsy — if you follow the link to my shop.

The site uses no advertising trackers and no cookies.

Your rights

You can clear the cart and the site's data in your browser settings at any time, and you can write to me to delete a message you sent earlier.
